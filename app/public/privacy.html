Off We Go Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026

Off We Go is a private trip-sharing service operated at offwego.to. This policy explains the information Off We Go handles and the controls available to you.

Information we process

  • Your email address, display name, profile picture, login sessions, and trip membership.
  • Precise location fixes when you explicitly enable location sharing, including while the app is in the background.
  • Photos you choose to upload, resized display copies and thumbnails, captions, capture times, and embedded or trail-matched locations.
  • Trip plans, routes, comments, likes, invitations, and basic security logs produced by the server.

How it is used

Information is used only to authenticate you, operate your private trips, place participants and selected photos on the trip map, send invitation email, prevent abuse, maintain backups, and diagnose service failures. Off We Go does not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

Who can see it

Trip content and location are available only to authenticated members of that trip. Owners control invitations. Infrastructure operators may access server data when necessary to administer, secure, back up, or repair the service.

Storage and retention

Off We Go stores its database and uploaded image copies on its privately controlled VPS. Full-resolution originals remain in your Apple Photos library. GPS fixes are automatically deleted after 30 days. Other trip and account data remains until it is deleted by you or a trip owner. Operational backups are normally retained for 14 days and then expire.

Service providers

Off We Go uses an OpenID Connect identity provider for account sign-in and an email provider to deliver trip invitations. Map tiles and geographic information may be requested from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data) and Wikipedia/Wikimedia, which receive ordinary network information such as your IP address under their own policies. Apple processes app distribution, Photos access, and device permission prompts.

Your controls

You can pause location sharing at any time, remove registered phones, delete uploaded content where your role permits, and permanently delete your account from the Account section inside Off We Go. Account deletion removes your profile, sessions, personal GPS history and uploads, and trips for which you are the sole owner.

Security and children

Off We Go uses encrypted HTTPS connections, standards-based account authentication, short-lived device-bound sign-in handoffs, private media URLs, access controls, and server backups. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Off We Go is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact privacy@threadway.ai or use the Off We Go support page.
