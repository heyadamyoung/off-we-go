Wayfare Support

Wayfare is the private map, photo, and live-location companion for your shared trips.

Sign-in

Use the email address invited to the trip. Sign in with your Wayfare ID, or create an account if you do not have one yet, then review and accept the trip invitation in Wayfare.

Location

Open the Phones section, register this iPhone, and enable sharing. Choose “Always Allow” when iOS offers it if you want updates while the screen is locked. You can pause sharing or remove the phone at any time.

Photos

Choose Add photo and select up to 20 items from Apple Photos. Wayfare uploads resized display copies; your original files stay in Apple Photos/iCloud.

Account deletion

Open the people/phones panel, scroll to Account, choose Delete my account, and type DELETE. If you cannot sign in, email support from the address used by your account.

Contact

Email support@threadway.ai. Include the device model, iOS version, approximate time of the problem, and the trip name; never send a password, sign-in handoff, or device token.
