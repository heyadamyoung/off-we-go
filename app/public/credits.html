Off We Go Map Credits

The maps in Off We Go are built from the following sources.

Map data

© OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.

Tiles and hosting

Vector tiles, fonts, and hosting by OpenFreeMap, using the OpenMapTiles schema (© OpenMapTiles, CC BY 4.0).

Cartographic design

The night and day map styles are adapted from basemap styles by CARTO, used under CC BY 4.0. Changes were made: the night style is repainted in Off We Go's palette, and both styles are retargeted to OpenFreeMap's tiles and fonts.

Other imagery

The home screen globe uses NASA Blue Marble imagery via NASA EOSDIS GIBS, with live cloud data from NOAA, EUMETSAT, and JMA. Sight descriptions and images come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, credited in place.
