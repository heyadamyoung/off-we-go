Off We Go Terms and Community Standards

Effective August 30, 2026

Off We Go is for private, invitation-only trip collaboration. You remain responsible for the plans, comments, photographs and other material you submit and must have the right to share it with the trip members you invite.

Not allowed

Do not upload or send unlawful, threatening, harassing, hateful, sexually exploitative, privacy-invasive, fraudulent, malicious, or copyright-infringing material. Do not use Off We Go to track someone without their knowledge and consent, expose another person’s precise location outside the invited group, interfere with the service, or attempt to access another trip.

Trip moderation

Trip owners can delete content, cancel invitations, and remove members and their reporting phones. Members should report safety or content concerns from the people panel or email safety@threadway.ai. Reports will be reviewed and content or access may be removed where appropriate.

Your account

You may stop location sharing or delete your account in the app. Access may be suspended for abuse, security threats, or violations of these standards. The service is provided as available without a guarantee that it will be uninterrupted.
